# Commercial use & licensing

**AetherForge is free for noncommercial use.**  
It is **not** free to monetize.

## What you may do without a commercial license

Under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE):

- Study, run, and modify AetherForge for personal, research, educational, hobby, or other **noncommercial** purposes  
- Share copies with the license and Required Notice intact  
- Build noncommercial internal research or public-knowledge experiments  

## What you may **not** do without a commercial license

Examples (non-exhaustive):

| Prohibited without commercial grant | Why |
|-------------------------------------|-----|
| Sell AetherForge or a rebranded fork | Monetization of the software |
| Offer AetherForge as a paid cloud / SaaS / “train your MoE” service | Commercial productization |
| Bundle AetherForge into a paid product, appliance, or enterprise suite | Commercial distribution |
| Charge for access, seats, or managed hosting of the factory | Monetization |
| Use AetherForge primarily to deliver paid customer deliverables where the software is the product | Commercial application |

Noncommercial organizations (charities, schools, public research labs, government institutions as defined in the license) have broader noncommercial-purpose rights — still not a blanket right to resell the software.

## Commercial licensing

If you need to:

- Sell or host AetherForge commercially  
- Embed it in a paid product

Email **admin@aetherawareness.com** with subject `AetherForge commercial license`,  
or open a GitHub issue tagged `commercial-license` on
